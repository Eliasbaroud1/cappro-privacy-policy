Getting started

  • Open CapPro. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. CapPro Pro is a subscription, and new subscribers start with a 3 day free trial on the Yearly plan.
  • Tap Generate in the Captions sheet to auto-transcribe your video's audio into captions.
  • Customize caption style, position, and animation, then export in the resolution you need.

Captions & translation

  • CapPro auto-generates captions from your video's audio using AI transcription.
  • Translate your captions into another language, or show two languages at once with bilingual captions.
  • If you close the app while captions or a translation are generating, reopen the project and CapPro resumes automatically.

Your data

  • Your videos and projects are stored on your device. To create captions, only the audio is sent for processing, and it is deleted as soon as your captions are ready.
  • See our Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? There is no sign-up, no email and no password. CapPro Pro is a subscription, so an active subscription or its free trial is required to use the app.
  • Can I edit the auto-generated captions? Yes. Tap any caption segment to edit its text or timing.
  • What resolutions can I export in? SD, HD, and 4K.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and choose CapPro. You can switch plan or cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before your period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • How do I switch to a different plan? Moving to a longer plan takes effect immediately, and Apple credits the unused part of your current plan. Use the Subscriptions screen in your iPhone Settings.
  • I subscribed on another device. How do I get access here? Open CapPro and tap Restore on the subscription screen. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID, not to one device.
  • How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • How do I delete my data? Deleting a project removes it from your device. To have anything held on our servers deleted, email info@barood.cc.
